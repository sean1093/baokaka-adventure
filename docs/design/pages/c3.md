## Changes from the outline (GDD § 4.4)

- **3-2.** The middle bike has no handlebar to pull: it is the 店員小哥's delivery bike (the chapter's cameo), and only its neighbours move it, shoving it across the path. Mocha on its seat holds it, and his landing rolls it back into the rack (a perch `set`). Two-way coupling between player-moved bikes would be a reaction cycle (lint L12) and makes every unheld first move a soft trap; this way the hold is still essential and no move ever has to be undone. The bell is a prop on a bike parked by the far palm.
- **3-3.** The 淘氣 becomes 「讓鵝對摩卡嘎嘎叫兩次」 (Mocha lands beside her twice while she sits). The goose's chase is the reveal: it happens as he crawls past her nest, one tick before he grabs the bead, so a chase around the lake can't be played as a 淘氣.
- **3-4.** Mocha's place is the gate's pillar: after 不要！ he leads Baokaka over the plate (a plain "get him moving" would send him back to Mocha at the start).
- **3-5.** Only the end shelves have cranks; each inner shelf rolls with its outer neighbour unless Mocha sits on it. In a pure shove system a lock only takes moves away, so "Mocha locks one shelf while you crank another" needs coupling; two-way coupling would be a reaction cycle and a field of soft traps. The returns desk makes the route *through* the stacks (in by aisle 1, out by aisle 3), so both pairs matter. 「Only one aisle opens at a time」 becomes "an aisle opens only when its shelves roll apart".
- **3-6.** He starts on the very page that becomes Mocha's bridge, and the page won't turn under him: that is what 不要！ is for (a decoy matters only while the treasure is hidden, and here Mocha alone reveals it). Her glasses leave with her (`heldBy`). Three flaps: his bookmark bridge, Mocha's page, the cat page.
