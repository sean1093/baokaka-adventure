## Changes from the outline (GDD § 4.4)

- **4-1.** The 淘氣 becomes 「一直說「不要！」，說到哇哇大哭」: 不要！ emits no event, so "three in a row" can't be counted; refusing the screen and then Mocha leaves him nothing to want, and the tantrum (a `cry`) is the dramatic head-shake. Mocha waits on the header over the doors (his place is the doorway, blocked while they are shut), so after 不要！ he stays at the doors, in 嗶！'s reach.
- **4-2.** Mocha doesn't push the lantern from inside: the roof window is his way up (it faces the street lamp in one state), and the lantern hangs from the east eave over the shutter's button. The house turns twice — once for Mocha, once for him — which keeps the page at three touchables and makes "align two doors" its last move.
- **4-3.** The 淘氣 becomes Mocha hiding behind the second sign when it lights (his shadow, enormous): with toggling signs, all three are never on at once, and Mocha stays hidden on this page so the lights are all he wants.
- **4-4.** The claw is two tabs — its cart and a drop **lever** (a prop is a discovery and never wears marigold, so a puzzle input can't be one). It needs no `carrier` (#120): the claw is a node on the cart, and his weight in it lifts it and sends it home. The coin slot sits on the machine's right panel, by the chute.
- **4-5.** The thing to refuse is the playing boombox itself, a decoy on its speaker stack (a light is the lowest want, so a light alone could never hold him against Mocha). Mocha starts out of 哇！'s reach (bring him closer first) and crosses over the frozen arch.
- **4-6.** The shutter is the stage's (it reveals the disco ball and opens the stage); the karaoke king sings while the lights are on and the lift follows them; the "three songs" are the lights coming on three times.
