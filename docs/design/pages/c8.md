## Changes from the outline (GDD § 4.4)

- **8-1.** The decoy is the car's stained-glass window over the middle seat; Mocha's lure is the far luggage rack past a 泡湯阿伯's bath bucket, and 抱抱 the 阿伯 lifts him over it. The 淘氣's "every seat" is remembered by the seats' cushions (buttons that keep his dent).
- **8-2.** Two vents, each a two-stop valve and a paper-cloud lift. The steam perch is the rock by the far vent: turn its steam off for Mocha, then back on to lift Baokaka's cloud beside him. The 淘氣's puff is the steam coming on under Mocha (D-77: a puff is not a page event).
- **8-3.** As outlined; the 阿嬤's hat is behind him at the steam's edge, so the 淘氣 needs Mocha to bring him back facing her.
- **8-4.** The pinwheel is `coupled` to the book lift (a loop, #127's `toy-pinwheel` pattern), and the window it sits outside must be opened first (`lockedWhen`). Mocha on the balcony rail is the lure that turns him to face it.
- **8-5.** Two tubs that drift with the level: half, full, half again. The 店員小哥's chapter 8 cameo is the bathhouse's shop counter, Mocha's goal across the drained bath. The 淘氣 floats Mocha's own (empty) tub out to him.
- **8-6.** The rods are sliders whose handles sit at the bottom of the page; the steam is on the counter and keeps Mocha off the bell on the 番台's desk (C7) until 咻—— blows it off; the bell opens the 番台's window in front of the 算盤.
