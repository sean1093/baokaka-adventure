The decoys draw as themselves (`AttractorDef.art`, D-79): the glowing phone, the shining griddle.

**Carriers** (GDD § 2.4 抱抱, #120): a grown-up who answers 抱抱 within one edge — her node, or one a baby edge joins to his — when she is `idle` (or `done`: then she carries him back), not `lockedWhen`, and every step of her `route` is open. She lifts him onto her node and walks the route one edge a tick; he decides again where she sets him down. A carrier's route may step along `climb` edges he can't use himself yet (he is lifted, not climbing): that is how a counter or a reclined chair is "a grown-up's step".

## Changes from the outline (GDD § 4.4)

- **5-1.** The decoy is a dozing passenger's phone, glowing in the dark car: it is visible only while the lights are off, and the bead only while they are on. The lights hang at the far end, so Mocha's lure and 不要！ bring him into 哇！'s reach first. The 淘氣 counts five switches of the lights.
- **5-2.** The prize shelf is dark and its bulbs are the third move (哇！ from up in the basket). Mocha starts on the stall's pole (his leap from it into the basket is one way, so he can't strand Baokaka up on the shelf). The 店員小哥 runs the stall (the chapter's cameo).
- **5-3.** The 雞排老闆 is in the back frying until Mocha rings the counter's bell (a button: he answers 抱抱 only while it is held). The 阿姨's twirl (the 淘氣) is a second hug: said at the counter before the 老闆 is called, she carries him back.
- **5-4.** Mocha leans on the launch knob (a button) rather than pushing a ball (a pushable is one way per attempt; a knob can be pressed again). The ball's state is a printed tab drawn as the board; its routes are the upper flipper (left lane or right lane to the out-hole) and the lower one (tray or jackpot).
- **5-5.** A stray dog naps on the second chair: 掰掰 sends it off, the route's last obstacle (two coins, 掰掰, 抱抱: par 4). The 淘氣's vibrating chair is chair 1, reclined.
- **5-6.** The vendor lifts him across the counter (her route is two nodes inside), and Mocha first lures him along the counter to her from the stall's sign; the counterweight is the tray stack itself, sinking under Mocha. The 蔥 is seen from inside the stall only.
