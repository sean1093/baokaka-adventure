## Changes from the outline (GDD § 4.4)

- **10-1.** The medley is three review words in the solution (嗶！ the door between the cars, 掰掰 a sleeping commuter's legs, 咻—— the balloons off the front window) and 哇！ in the 淘氣. The outline's 淘氣 「use every word once」 needs a tracker of seven flags (×128 states) for a page that is a warm-up: it is 「對著窗外的101說哇！」 instead. The 「ㄅㄚ！」 at the view is the card's third line, played as 101 appears.
- **10-2.** As outlined. The 清潔阿伯 carries him over a `walk` edge (the open floor to the window), foreshadowing 10-6; the 淘氣 counts trips without him, which can happen here (unlike 1-5).
