## Changes from the outline (GDD § 4.4)

- **2-2.** Mocha starts in view on the park lamp, so the first move is to hide him in the 店員小哥's crate (C9): a light (priority 4) never beats Mocha (priority 2), which makes the leash's rule visible and gives the page its second move (par 2 for difficulty 2). It also places the chapter's 店員小哥 cameo (§ 5.3). Mocha's reply to him is 「……又是你？」 (a callback to 1-5).
- **2-3.** The dog sleeps *on* the bead (it is hidden until the dog leaves), and Mocha on the bench is what he wants at first. That keeps the solution's 掰掰 out of the turtle's reach, so the 淘氣 takes a deliberate trip back across with Mocha instead of coming free with the solution.
- **2-4.** "Flees to the branch farthest from Mocha" is read as "when Mocha lands next to it": the east fork sends it west, the west fork sends it down, and Mocha settling anywhere else while it is low sends it back up. Three laps = three herdings down.
- **2-5.** The 淘氣 「Tap Mocha's wet perch three times」 needs a puff to count as a page event, which the engine doesn't have (`mochaPuff` is a UI event; mischief predicates name § 3.2 events). The C7 beat stays — tapping the wet stone puffs him up — and the 淘氣 becomes four rides for the egret on the turning pad.
- **2-6.** The three flaps are a bridge for Baokaka, a branch for Mocha and the portrait corner (the 土地公's hiding place); two are needed, and all three open is the 淘氣.
