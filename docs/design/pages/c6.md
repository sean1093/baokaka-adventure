## Changes from the outline (GDD § 4.4)

- **6-1.** One switch tab, and the train is a printed state part 嗶！ starts (a driverless train is a robot, § 2.4): the loop branch comes back to the junction, and throwing the switch on the loop sends the train on to the airport. The passenger is a cabin-crew 姊姊 dozing by the doors; she lifts him over the step onto the platform.
- **6-2.** The start button latches the beacon, and the belt is turned by hand once it spins; the 店員小哥 minds the hall (the chapter's cameo).
- **6-3.** The pigeons on the gate's button open it as they lift off (a `sky` reaction on the gate); Mocha's lure to the window is the page's first move.
- **6-4.** The roosting birds sit on the cab's gangplank (the seesaw): they lock the cab's swing while it drags on the apron, and are needed back to tip it onto the door's sill — both halves of 飛機！. Mocha has no part in it.
- **6-5.** The pigeons doze on the catwalk round the crate (blocking his way and Mocha's hiding place) rather than on the window ledges; the searchlight's pool lands on the railing, so he follows it reaching; 不要！ to Mocha is the second way past him.
- **6-6.** 嗶！ wakes the X-ray scanner before the belt turns; the pigeons doze in the tray at the belt's mouth.
